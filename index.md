# Hi, I'm Aarush.

### Computing Science Student & Accessibility Engineer

I am a student of the subtle and beautiful arts of computing science, exploring software engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and systems architecture at the University of Alberta.

Technology intersects with almost every aspect of modern life. For me, it is the primary mechanism I use to navigate and reshape the world. Driven by a lifelong journey of breaking systemic barriers, I believe that technology, if utilized with thoughtfulness, has the potential to dismantle exclusion. My work focuses heavily on the intersection of Technical Innovation and Accessibility, building tools to ensure the generation after me does not have to face the same barriers I did.
